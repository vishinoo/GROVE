Modes

One word that sets the room up. A playlist, a video in the background, the sites and apps you work in, opened together and closed together.

Your modes

Colour

Changing it here changes it on your phone too. It is the same field on both, so they stay a matching pair.

Saying it

Say "study mode" to ⌘K or to Grove on your phone and it starts. "End study mode" closes what it opened: its own browser window, the apps it launched, and the music it put on. Nothing you already had open is touched.
